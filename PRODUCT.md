# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Graph-layout implementers and Stately engineers validating layout behavior before integrating it into Viz. They need to compare algorithms, inspect exact geometry, and identify renderer-vs-layout discrepancies quickly.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy, graph-native TypeScript layout engine and parity lab. Success means ELK-compatible behavior is measurable, native and oracle-backed cases are labeled honestly, and the same serialized `@statelyai/graph` geometry can be inspected without renderer normalization.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, candid. The interface should feel like an engineering instrument: dense enough for expert work, calm enough for sustained comparison, and explicit about provenance and limitations.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a marketing dashboard, decorative node editor, or bespoke graph-format showcase. Avoid hiding dimensions, routes, ports, or algorithm provenance behind renderer-specific abstractions. Do not imply parity where an oracle snapshot is still used.

## Design Principles

1. Show the authoritative geometry.
2. Compare renderers against one shared graph value.
3. Make provenance and fidelity visible.
4. Prefer inspectable measurements over decoration.
5. Keep graph data native to `@statelyai/graph`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for engine or geometry meaning. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion behavior. Keep exact values available as text, not only visual marks.
